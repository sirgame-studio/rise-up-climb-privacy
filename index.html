Rise Up Climb Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Who we are

Rise Up Climb (Android package com.riseupclimb) is a game published by Sirgame Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Contact: sirgame1591@gmail.com.

The game is bundled inside the app and runs locally on your device. You do not need an account.

Information stored only on your device

The app saves your best score, game settings (such as sound on or off) and whether you have seen the in-app privacy notice. This data is stored in the app's local storage on your device and is not sent to us. It stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads between some rounds, and full-screen ads when you open or return to the app. The app requests non-personalised ads only, and it does not request access to your Android advertising ID.

When ads are requested or shown, Google's Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares data with Google, as Google describes in its data disclosure:

DataExamplesPurposes
Device and other identifiersApp set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Approximate locationDerived from IP addressAdvertising, fraud prevention
App activityAd views, taps and other ad interactionsAdvertising, analytics
DiagnosticsCrash logs, performance and SDK error informationAnalytics, fraud prevention

This data is sent over encrypted connections. Google and its partners may use it to show and measure ads, limit repeated ads, and detect fraud and abuse. Non-personalised ads are based on context, such as the app and your approximate location, rather than on your past behaviour. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Privacy notice. When you first open the app, a notice explains how ads work in the app. Ads are not requested until you select Continue.
  • Read this policy in the app. Tap the privacy button in the top-left corner of the game screen at any time.
  • Advertising ID. The app does not request your advertising ID. You can also reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path varies by device).
  • Delete local game data. Android Settings → Apps → Rise Up Climb → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

What we do not do

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location. It has no account, chat or leaderboard, and it does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own. We do not sell personal information.

Children

Rise Up Climb is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us.

Data retention and security

Local game data stays on your device until you clear it. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policy. Game files are loaded from inside the app and are not downloaded from the internet.

Third-party services

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) – advertising.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this page. GitHub may log standard request data when you visit it. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the app's features, advertising or data practices change, and change the effective date above.
